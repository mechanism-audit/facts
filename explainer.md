# Coldread: product audit and mechanism verification, in plain terms

**A five-day written audit at a fixed price: one careful visitor walks your live product the way a paying customer would and writes down what is real, what is not, and what to fix first.**

Coldread is a fixed-price, five-day written audit that traces every promise on a product's pages to the code or process that actually keeps it, and returns a ranked defect list with a keep, repair or rebuild decision.

It is built and run by Jacaranda Labs in Harare, Zimbabwe. Live: https://mechanism-audit.whop.site

## The short answer

It is a five-day written audit of a live product: one careful visitor walks it as a paying customer would and traces every promise on the site to the code or process that actually keeps it.

## Who it is for

Founders and product teams that shipped fast and cannot tell what is real.

Markets: Global.

## How it works

**Kickoff.** Scope agreed in writing.

**Walk it as a stranger.** Sign-up, the core flow and the edges, as a paying customer would.

**Ten checks.** The recurring ways a product only looks complete.

**Written up.** Findings in writing, ranked.

**Delivered.** A keep, repair or rebuild decision at the end.

**Fix Sprint.** The repairs themselves, after the audit.

## What you get

- Claims with no page or code behind them, listed
- Template residue removed from the story
- A ranked defect list
- A written keep, repair or rebuild decision
