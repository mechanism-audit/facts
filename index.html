Questions

What is a Coldread?It is a five-day written audit of a live product: one careful visitor walks it as a paying customer would and traces every promise on the site to the code or process that actually keeps it.
How much does it cost?The Coldread is $450, the Fix Sprint that follows it is $1,450, and the Self-Check Pack is $19 for teams that want to run the ten checks themselves first.
How long does it take?Five days from kickoff to a written decision.
What do I receive?A written report with a ranked list of defects, the claims that have nothing behind them, and a keep, repair or rebuild recommendation.
Who is it for?Founders and small teams that shipped quickly with AI assistance and need to know which parts of the product, and of the story about it, are real.
Does someone need access to my code?The audit is run against the live product as a customer experiences it, which is the surface where an unkept promise shows.
